# Privacy

VaganciaFitness and VaganciaFitnessDebug store their records in their own local app storage. Workout records can include timing, goals, received treadmill metrics, and notes. Diagnostic records can include Bluetooth service information, original protocol bytes, errors, timestamps, and operator notes. The Apple Watch companion can handle workout and health data through HealthKit and WatchConnectivity, subject to the permissions and actions chosen on the device.

The apps do not automatically upload diagnostic records to a project server. A person must explicitly create an export and choose a destination or recipient through the system sharing interface. Exports can include detailed and potentially identifying information; review them before sharing. The workout share card is also generated locally and shared only after an explicit share action.

The source provides complete and public-support ZIP profiles. Complete exports retain original evidence. Public-support exports omit event payloads, notes, HealthKit values, raw Watch messages and persistent identities and use relative times and archive-local IDs. They retain only a narrow list of known GATT UUIDs. This limited metadata projection cannot replace complete evidence for every investigation and does not guarantee anonymization for every input. Personal share cards contain the selected workout's values. [Diagnostic contracts](shared/contracts/diagnostic-export/README.md) define the projection; [software checks](docs/testing/software.md) describe its sentinel coverage.

See [diagnostic sharing details](docs/privacy/sharing-diagnostics.md).

The apps and FitnessKit resource bundles include `PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy` manifests. They declare no tracking or developer collection and record the required-reason APIs actually used: elapsed-time measurements, app-owned preferences and, for the iPhone runtime, app-container file timestamps used by diagnostic storage. These declarations do not replace HealthKit authorization or describe an export as non-sensitive. `apple/scripts/check-privacy` checks source declarations; its `--built-products` option also checks the packaged app and package resources. Reasons follow [Apple's required-reason API definitions](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/bundleresources/app-privacy-configuration/nsprivacyaccessedapitypes/nsprivacyaccessedapitype); review declarations whenever an API use or data flow changes.
