# Community conduct

We want participation in VAGANCIA FITNESS to be respectful, clear, and focused on the work. Be considerate of different backgrounds and experience levels. Critique ideas and code without personal attacks, harassment, discriminatory language, or unwanted contact.

Maintainer: the project is maintained by one individual. Use the repository's GitHub Issues to report conduct concerns, linking the relevant public project discussion where possible. Keep personal or sensitive information out of public reports. The maintainer reviews reports when able and may remove content or restrict participation when needed.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is representing the project. It is not a guarantee of response time or ongoing support.
